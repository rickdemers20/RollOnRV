The Logbook — every job, in order

Tap a line to read the whole story. Tap Log a job and the camera opens first — the photo starts the entry.

Checklists — before you roll

Big rows, one thumb, works with no signal. Pick the moment you're in.

Tires, by age — not tread

Blowouts come from old tires, not worn tread. Enter each DOT code once; the book watches the birthdays. Flags start at 5 years, turn serious at 7. The bar fills toward the 10-year hard limit.

Parts to get — before the job

What you need, which job it's for, and what you paid when you've got it. It lives in the app — print it for the counter, or download it as a file.

My pros — the shops you trust

Shops and techs you trust, with a number to call. Tick "Leave it to the pro" on a task and pick one — the number rides along with the job.

Fuel — what she drinks

Every fill-up, with the miles it covered. Fill the tank each time and the MPG stays honest.

Trips — where she's been

Where you went, when, and what it cost. The fuel inside the trip's dates counts itself toward the total.

The shops and techs you trust — name, what they do, phone, town. Pick one from a task when the job belongs to a shop.

Bring your list in — or take it out

Import a task list from a spreadsheet (CSV). Columns it understands: title, category, due date, due mileage, due generator hours, every-months, note. It also reads a simple "task, category, due date" file.

Premium — a drawer for later

The free core is the whole book, and it stays free. Later updates will add the ownership report (one clean PDF for a buyer), the document vault (registration, insurance, warranties with expiry alerts), parts & warranty tracking, more than one rig, and backup. Nothing here asks you to pay today.
